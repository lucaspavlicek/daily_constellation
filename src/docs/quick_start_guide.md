Every day, one of the eighty-eight IAU constellations is chosen at random. Your job is to guess it in the fewest turns. Don't worry, with each incorrect guess you will be shown a star map to help narrow it down. Make your first guess within the sidebar on the left, then after a few seconds, a plot should appear.

![Plot shown after guess 'Aries'](/app/static/images/guess_1_plot.png)

This plot is a real view of the sky from somewhere on Earth, and will *always* contain both the guessed constellation *and* the mystery constellation. Notice that Aries is on the plot and marked grey. The correct constellation is also somewhere on there, and marked green. The constellations marked red can be ruled out. The colors will be handy to keep track of things after multiple turns.

A left, down, and right arrow button will have appeared on the sidebar. Use the down arrow to see the other half of the sky.

![Nadir plot after guessing Aries](/app/static/images/nadir_plot.png)

This plot shows the what the sky on the other side of the Earth looks like. Together, the two plots show the complete celestial sphere.

So congrats, we have ruled out about half the eighty-eight constellations. Keep guessing to narrow it down further.

![Example plot after turn 3](/app/static/images/turn_3_plot.png)

Here we are two turns later. We can see our past guess on the bottom of the sidebar. The plot also has more colors now. The red constellations have shown up in zero out of three plots. The darkest green constellations have shown up in all three. An intermediate color means that constellation has shown up in some, but not all three, of the star maps. *Note that the mystery constellation will always show up in every plot, and will therefore always be dark green.*

The left arrow button in the sidebar has become enabled, and the arrow buttons can be used to navigate between star maps from previous turns.

Next, notice that the dropdown of constellations to guess is changing.

![Example of dropdown menu](app/static/images/dropdown.png)

The numbers in the brackets tell us how many of our star plots the constellation has shown up in. Once again, the mystery constellation will show up in every one, so in this case, the mystery constellation will be one of those that says \[3/3\]. That being said, it may be worth guessing constellation that have *not* shown up every time in the early turns to narrow down the possibilities faster.

Additionally, the constellations we have already guessed will be removed from the options, so we must guess a new constellation every turn.

Keep guessing, and looking over the plots, and soon, the number of remaining possibilities will be just a few.
